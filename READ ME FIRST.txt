MIRAKLE WEBSITE - UPDATE 9
Archivo typeface, "Architectural" naming, no vertical colours, and the recorded demo voice.

1. In Chrome open:  https://github.com/miraklelightings/mirakle-site/upload/main
2. Inside this folder, select the four items together: the "src" folder, the "public" folder,
   package.json and package-lock.json. Do NOT select this read-me.
3. Drag the four onto the page together, wait for the list to finish, then press "Commit changes".
4. Wait three minutes and open https://mirakle-site-66p3.vercel.app/quiklit/boq/
